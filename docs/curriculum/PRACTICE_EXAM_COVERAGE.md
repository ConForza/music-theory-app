# Practice examination coverage and variation

> *Generated by `npm run audit:prep -w @mtp/api -- --write`. Do not edit by hand.*

Each grade's practice examination is planned from 6 fixed seeds, each for a fresh learner, from the real curriculum. Product defaults used: 2 questions per outcome, 4 score-reading questions, 90 s per question for the optional timer (a product default, not an ABRSM time allowance). Nothing here describes ABRSM's own papers.

Mean overlap is the share of one paper's questions (same music and answer, whatever the seed) that reappear in another paper: low means a new paper is genuinely new. "Templates differ" is how often the same position in two papers asks a different kind of question.
