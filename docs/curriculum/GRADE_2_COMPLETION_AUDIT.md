## 7. Answer checks

The generators write their own answers and the engine's solvability gate grades those same answers, which is circular for choice questions. `packages/engine/test/grade2-answers.test.ts` therefore re-derives each new Grade 2 answer from what the learner is shown (the printed notation and the prompt) with the curriculum rule functions, over many seeds per template:

- beats, beat note, duple/triple/quadruple and the single note that fills a bar: from the printed time signature and exact note-value arithmetic;
- triplet questions: from the printed groups (group length, notes played, beat position, units adding to three; a rest is never counted as a note);
- rest and grouping questions: every one of the three bars is judged by the bar-rule checker, and exactly one may be free of issues; no dotted rests appear;
- ledger-line and clef questions: from the clef tables (`staffPosition`, `ledgerLines`);
- scale gaps, key signatures, relative keys, degrees, tonic triads and intervals: from `scale`, `keySignature`, `relativeKey` for major keys and harmonic minor; the 7th of a minor key is never asked by number;
- terms: from the vocabulary table, and no near-synonym is offered beside the target;
- melody questions: from the printed bars (bar totals, tuplet bars, the marked note) and the key.

Hand-typed anchors in the same file: the notes of A, B♭ and E♭ major; A, E and D harmonic minor; the key signatures; the relative of D minor; the tonic triads of E♭ major, D minor and E minor.

The wider course tests (`grade2-course.test.ts`) also check that every question in every practice, review, challenge and assessment run lies inside the taught scope of its node and credits at least one concept, that no lesson repeats a question, and that melodic minor is never visible to the learner.

## 8. Known limitations

- No qualified teacher has reviewed any of this; every template, lesson and term list is a draft.
- Teaching quality is untested with real beginners. Sound is synthesised only.
- The Grade 2 term list is the provisional vocabulary (Q2): eight terms first used at Grade 2 (presto, largo, vivace, grazioso, maestoso, sforzando, fortepiano, tenuto).
- Melodies in context are in major keys only; a minor-key melody question is a teacher decision.
- The natural-minor pattern is taught as a stepping stone to the harmonic form and is asked only as tone/semitone steps, never as a scale to write. Melodic minor is deferred to Grade 3 and visible only in the optional preview.
- The rule checker accepts both two beat rests and a minim rest for a silent half of a 4/4 bar; the lesson teaches the minim rest as the neat form.
- Dotted rests, duplets and the 7th of a minor key by number are not asked at Grade 2.
- The pass marks and mastery thresholds are the provisional values from Briefs 04 and 05 (not changed).
